The Sun: our star

The Sun is the closest star to Earth. Its light and heat provide many of the conditions that life on our planet needs.

The Sun is made mostly of hydrogen and helium. In its core, nuclear fusion turns hydrogen into helium and releases energy. It is not a bonfire: its energy is produced in a different way.

Sunlight takes about eight minutes to reach Earth. This light also illuminates the Moon and the planets.

Never look directly at the Sun. Ordinary sunglasses do not protect your eyes for solar viewing. This activity involves looking at shadows without looking toward the Sun.

An activity to share

Place an object on the ground in a safe location. Draw its shadow in the morning and afternoon. Record the time and compare the direction and size of the shadows.
