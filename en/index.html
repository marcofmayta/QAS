YOUR FIRST JOURNEY STARTS HERE

Tonight, look up.

You do not need a telescope to begin. Share a moment under the stars with your family or community, and keep a notebook of discoveries.

  1. Find a safe place with little artificial light and a clear sky. Bring warm clothing and company.
  2. Let your eyes adjust to the dark for about 20 minutes. Avoid looking at your screen.
  3. Draw what you see. Record the time, place, and words your family uses to name the sky.
