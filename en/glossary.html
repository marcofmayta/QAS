Words for learning astronomy

Astronomy studies bodies in space and phenomena in the universe. This glossary explains words used in QAS articles.

These definitions describe scientific concepts. When translating them, a short explanation can be used if the community has no commonly used equivalent word.

Star
A body of hot gas that produces energy inside it. The Sun is a star.
Planet
In the solar system, a nearly spherical body that orbits the Sun, is not a moon, and gravitationally dominates its orbital neighborhood.
Orbit
The path of a body around another under the influence of gravity.
Gravity
Attraction between bodies that have mass. It keeps planets in orbit around the Sun.
Natural satellite
A natural body that orbits a planet or another larger body. The Moon orbits Earth.
Galaxy
A collection of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.
Constellation
A defined region of the sky. It is often associated with an imagined pattern of stars that may be far apart from each other.
Light-year
The distance light travels in one year. It is a unit of distance, not time.
Nebula
A cloud of gas and dust in space. Stars form in some nebulae.
Atmosphere
A layer of gases surrounding a body, such as Earth.
