Languages and the sky

Inti and Willka are words associated with the Sun in Quechua and Aymara. Killa and Phaxsi name the Moon; Qoyllur and Wara wara name stars. Spellings and usage may vary across communities.

QAS brings astronomy and Indigenous languages together. There is no single Andean perspective: communities have their own voices, experiences, and ways of sharing knowledge.

This version includes articles in four languages. The Quechua and Aymara translations are drafts generated with AI assistance. They still need review and adaptation by community speakers.

An activity to share

Try this together: ask a family member which words they use for the sky. With their permission, record the word, meaning, and community or language variety. Do not publish stories or recordings without permission.
