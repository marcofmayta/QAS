The solar system

The solar system consists of the Sun and the bodies orbiting it. The Sun is a star; planets shine mainly by reflecting its light.

Moving outward from the Sun, the eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The first four are rocky planets. Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants; Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

Saturn’s rings contain countless pieces of ice and rock. They may look solid in illustrations, but they consist of particles of different sizes.

There are also moons, asteroids, comets, and dwarf planets such as Pluto. Our Moon reflects sunlight. Its phases change with the relative positions of Earth, the Moon, and the Sun.

An activity to share

Activity: draw the Moon over several nights and record each date. Never look directly at the Sun or view it through binoculars or telescopes without suitable solar filters.
