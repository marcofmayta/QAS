QAS · Quechua Aimara Space

One shared sky. Many voices.

QAS — Quechua Aimara Space — is an educational project bringing space closer to communities in Puno and across Peru. It is for students, families, and curious people of all ages.

Explore astronomy, discover vocabulary, and download a guide to observing the sky without a telescope.

You can read the articles in Spanish, English, Quechua, and Aymara. The Quechua and Aymara versions are initial translations. Review with local speakers and adaptation to their language varieties are still pending.
